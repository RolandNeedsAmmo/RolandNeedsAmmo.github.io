FACTION 01

1. Knights of the Round (Lords: King Arthur & Queen Guinevere)

FACTION OBJECTIVE
ONCE AND FUTURE KING
TRACK
0123

Advancement

Advance 1 space each time you COMPLETE A PROPHECY or OBTAIN THE GRAIL or EXCALIBUR.

Completion

UNLOCK KING ARTHUR from the reserve and flip the Lord board to Side B.

Longbows & Chivalry System

Longbows

Knights' Settlements have 2 Strength each.

Chivalry

Free action, at any time add a banner to a Champion. That Champion becomes that Knight for the rest of the game. The banner can no longer be removed.

Knight Abilities

Sir Galahad - The Grail Knight (reaction)

When you win a battle in a Region with an Artifact present (including the Grail): GAIN 2 VP FOR EACH ARTIFACT owned by the player (including the Artifact you would win in the battle).

Sir Gawain - Kingdom Advance

MOVE X UNITS from Sir Gawain's region to other adjacent regions (including the same region). Free if you have a Settlement in this region.

Sir Percival - Slay the Beast (reaction)

When a Mythic Beast is controlled by an enemy: DEPLOY (or MOVE) SIR PERCIVAL and 2 WARRIORS into that region.

Sir Lancelot - Legendary Duel

In a region with an enemy Champion: GAIN 1 FAVOR and DECLARE A NUMBER (1-3). The enemy must discard a card with that Initiative number or the enemy Champion is destroyed (return it to its Reserve).

BGG FAQ: SIR LANCELOT DUEL

The player controlling the Knights chooses which Hero to destroy if there is more than one.

Lord Abilities

TO ARMS (reaction) Guinevere

Before any battle in a region where you have at least 1 Strength: SPEND 1 POWER to Add +2 Strength (you may max match the highest enemy Strength value, but not exceed it). This Ability can be used only ONCE PER SEASON in any battle, not just the one involving Queen Guinevere.

CALL OF THE KING King Arthur

He is considered a Lord with 3 Strength.

SPEND 1 POWER to Move X Units from regions adjacent to King Arthur's region.

Lord Artifact Assignment

Excalibur and the Grail must be assigned to King Arthur or Guinevere, increasing their Strength. They do NOT occupy an Artifact Slot; place them beside the board.

FACTION 02

2. The Undead (Lord: Mordred & Mordred Ascendant)

FACTION OBJECTIVE
MORDRED ASCENDANT
TRACK
012345

Advancement

Advance 1 space when you BUILD SETTLEMENTS, OBTAIN AN ARTIFACT (excluding the initial one), or Mordred GAINS A +1 STRENGTH TOKEN.

Completion

Flip the Lord board to Side B.

ALTARS OF BONES (SETTLEMENTS)

ALTARS OF BONES

If you win a battle in a region where you have NO Settlements: BUILD A SETTLEMENT in that region. If there is no free slot, BUILD IT ANYWAY. GAIN PLACEMENT BONUSES, ADVANCE 1 SPACE on the Objective Track. Remember: max 1 Settlement per player per region.

Faction Abilities

Reanimate (passive)

When you deploy into a Region with one of your Settlements: DEPLOY 1 EXTRA WARRIOR into that Region (max 1 additional Warrior per region per Turn).

Corrupt Land

After playing any card with a Build Settlement icon: BUILD 1 EXTRA SETTLEMENT in an Explored region where you have at least 1 Strength, occupying a slot without a bonus (max 1 Settlement per player per region). GAIN THE PLACEMENT BONUSES AGAIN and ADVANCE AGAIN on the Objective Track. If slot unavailable, do not build the new Settlement.

Shamble

MOVE X UNITS from a Region with one of your Settlements to any Explored Region.

Consume Magical Essence (reaction)

When you gain control of a Mythic Beast: GAIN ITS VP but DO NOT place your flag. Discard the card, remove the miniature from the game and Mordred GAINS A +1 STRENGTH TOKEN and ADVANCES 1 SPACE on the Objective Track (BGG FAQ: Misprint — it is considered a Reaction Ability).

Unholy Dominion

GAIN 1 VP for each of your Settlements on Avalon.

Raise Undead Horde Once per Turn

DEPLOY 1 UNIT in an Explored region with one of your Settlements (remove the cube between Turns and pay the cost each time you use it).

Lord Abilities

LORD OF DEATH (reaction) Mordred Ascendant

If the Faction Objective was completed at the beginning of the War Phase: after awarding the points for a Region where Mordred is present, DESTROY 2 OF YOUR UNITS in any Explored region and DEPLOY MORDRED in that region (he may fight there again).

ENMITY OF THE SHATTERED SON (passive) Mordred

When Mordred is in a region with an enemy Hero: Mordred GAINS +1 STRENGTH (he does NOT gain a +1 Strength Token).

Lord Artifact Assignment

Excalibur and the Grail must be assigned to Mordred, increasing his Strength. They do NOT occupy an Artifact Slot; place them beside the board.

FACTION 03

3. Druids (Lord: Merlin)

FACTION OBJECTIVE
SACRED SITE

Advancement

BUILD 6 LEY LINES on Avalon.

Completion

Flip the Lord board to Side B. If your Settlement is on Avalon, REPLACE IT with the ANCIENT ONE (removing your Settlement from the game). Otherwise, if your Settlement is not on Avalon, you CANNOT GAIN the Ancient One.

LEY LINES & ANCIENT ONE

LEY LINES

As often and whenever you want during your turn, SPEND 1 POWER or DISCARD 1 SETTLE CARD to BUILD 1 LEY LINE (the topmost one) in any Explored Region - even the CENTRAL REGION - where you have at least 1 Strength and doesn't already have a Ley Line. If you have Ley Lines everywhere, place it where you already have other Ley Lines. They do NOT occupy a Settlement slot. They count as 1 Strength in the Region.

GAIN PLACEMENT BONUSES each time you build a Ley Line (Warriors must be deployed in the Region with the Ley Line just built). GAIN PLACEMENT BONUSES during the Mist Phase as well (in this case, all Warriors must be deployed in the Region with your “only” Settlement; if it is not present, you CANNOT deploy them).

ANCIENT ONE

Special Unit with 3 Strength. It is considered a Mythic Beast, Settlement and Unit at the same time. It MOVES like any Unit and allows you to DEPLOY UNITS like any Settlement. Any +1 Strength Token on the Settlement transfers to the Ancient One when it replaces the Settlement on Avalon. It CANNOT be destroyed, does NOT count toward the Mythic Beast limit BUT DOES COUNT toward the Unit limit (max 5).

Faction Abilities

Travel The Ways (passive)

When deploying Units ONLY during the Preparation Phase, your Ley Lines in Explored Regions count as Settlements. SPEND 1 POWER to extend the ability to unexplored regions.

Master Of The Mists

MOVE MERLIN from any region to any region. By moving this way, he can explore.

The Raven Summons

DEPLOY 2 UNITS in a Region with Merlin or one of your Settlements.

Astral Convergence

MOVE X UNITS from Adjacent Regions or from Any Regions with your Ley Lines INTO a Region with your Settlement.

Lord Abilities

ANCIENT SEER Once per Season (reaction) Merlin

After drawing a Combat Card, choose whether to DISCARD IT AND DRAW ANOTHER or ADD 1 BONUS CARD. Either action could help you NOT FAIL the Battle Plan. This Ability can be used only ONCE PER SEASON in any battle, not just the one involving Merlin.

In Deterministic Mode, you may only choose to add 1 Bonus Card.

VORTEX OF POWER Merlin

SPEND 2 POWER (1 POWER in a 2-player game) and DESTROY 1 ENEMY WARRIOR of your choice in each Region adjacent to the Region where your Settlement/Ancient One is located.

Lord Artifact Assignment

Excalibur and the Grail must be assigned to Merlin, increasing his Strength. They do NOT occupy an Artifact Slot; place them beside the board.

4. The Fomorians (Lord: Balor)

FACTION OBJECTIVE
SUMMON THE LEVIATHAN
TRACK
0123

Advancement

Advance 1 space when you WIN A BATTLE in an Unexplored Region or BUILD A SETTLEMENT anywhere.

Completion

UNLOCK THE LEVIATHAN from the reserve and flip the Lord board to Side B.

TURNING THE TIDE, SUNKEN SETTLEMENTS & LEVIATHAN

THE TIDE

At the end of each of your Turns, MOVE THE TIDE MARKER to the next position (High > Mid > Low > High > Mid...). You have access ONLY to the abilities matching the Tide marker's current position. Once per Turn: SPEND X POWER to MOVE THE TIDE MARKER X SPACES, in any direction (max 1 passive Tide ability per Turn).

SUNKEN SETTLEMENTS

Regions with your Settlements ALSO COUNT AS COASTAL REGIONS in addition to their Base Type (except for Quest Cards and Prophecies). In addition, you may SPEND 1 POWER to MOVE X UNITS from any Coastal Region.

LEVIATHAN

Special Unit with 2 Strength. You may DEPLOY IT ONLY IN THE SEA, choosing a Cardinal Point. It ADDS ITS STRENGTH to the 3 Regions in the corresponding Cardinal Quadrant (if you have a Settlement in one of those three regions, also consider the Central Region). It CANNOT be destroyed, does NOT count toward the Mythic Beast limit BUT does count toward the Unit limit (max 5). During the Mist Phase it is removed, like all other Units (exception: the Druids' Ancient One, because it is also considered a Settlement).

Faction Abilities

From the Deep (passive)

Immediately before resolving any Card with a Deploy Unit icon: DEPLOY AN EXTRA UNIT in that Coastal Region.

Tsunami

DESTROY UP TO 3 ENEMY WARRIORS in a Coastal Region.

Surge (passive)

Immediately before resolving any Card: GAIN 1 BONUS based on its Initiative (1 = Move 1 Unit | 2 = Gain 1 Favor | 3 = Gain 1 Power).

Despoil (reaction)

When you Explore, GAIN ALL EXPLORATION BONUSES instead of 1 (note: you cannot gain 1 Power as an Exploration Bonus and then use it immediately to activate this ability, because it is a Reaction).

Scavenge Once per Turn (passive)

When you GAIN FAVOR/POWER/CARD, you may gain 4 VP/Power/Card instead.

Encroach

GAIN 1 DISCOUNT for the Settle card you are playing OR DISCARD A CARD to gain 2 VP and also a +1 Strength Token on one of your Settlements that does not have one.

Lord Abilities

ADAPTION (reaction) Balor

When a Battle Plan Card is discarded by you or an enemy in any battle: GAIN ONE OF THESE CARDS. This Ability can be used only ONCE PER SEASON in any battle, not just the one involving Balor.

Lord Artifact Assignment

Excalibur and the Grail must be assigned to Balor, increasing his Strength. They do NOT occupy an Artifact Slot; place them beside the board.

FACTION OBJECTIVE
REPEL THE INVADERS
TRACK
01234

Advancement

Advance 1 space when you GAIN A PYRRHIC VICTORY or USE “ENTHRALL” on an enemy Warrior.

Completion

Flip the Lord board to Side B.

PYRRHIC VICTORY, ENTHRALL & ASPECTS OF NATURE

PYRRHIC VICTORY (passive)

In every Battle where you have at least 1 Strength and the Strength difference between first and second place for the Victory is 0 or 1: GAIN 3 EXTRA VP in addition to any VP gained.

ENTHRALL (passive)

When you Move an Enemy Warrior into an Unexplored Region with one of your Warriors or one of your Settlements: PLACE THE ENEMY WARRIOR ON YOUR BOARD. It now counts as a “THRALL”. This passive ability works only in combination with the “Dreamweaver” and/or “Fey Enchantment” abilities. At the beginning of the War Phase: PLACE EACH “THRALL” WARRIOR on your board in any Explored Region, under the control of that Faction's player, provided that player does not already have more than 5 Units. Additional “Thralls” go to the respective players' Reserves.

ASPECTS OF NATURE (passive)

Your Champions CANNOT EQUIP ARTIFACTS. They also represent the 3 Territory Types (Champion 1: Forests, Champion 2: Mountains, Champion 3: Rivers). At the beginning of the War Phase: each of your Champions GAINS A +1 STRENGTH TOKEN if their type matches the Territory of the Region where they are deployed. They can accumulate Strength Tokens during the game.

Faction Abilities

Movement In The Mists Once per Turn

DEPLOY 2 UNITS OR MOVE X UNITS from one Region to other adjacent Regions (including the same region).

Dreamweaver Once per Turn

In a Region where you have at least 1 Strength: MOVE 1 ENEMY WARRIOR into an Adjacent Region. This may activate the “Enthrall” ability.

Fey Enchantment Once per Turn

PERFORM “ENTHRALL” ON AN ENEMY WARRIOR in Morgana's Region, even if it is Unexplored, and PLACE IT ON YOUR BOARD.

Glamor

Place a Faction cube on an Enemy Warrior (1 per Faction), in a Region where you have at least 1 Strength: CONTROL THESE UNITS until the end of the War Phase, after which you remove the cubes.

Lord Abilities

DEEP MAGIC Once per Battle Morgana

REDRAW A COMBAT CARD (the one just drawn) OR TAKE A USED BONUS CARD BACK INTO YOUR HAND if you win the Battle. This Ability can be used in MULTIPLE BATTLES DURING THE SEASON, not just in the Battle involving Morgana.

QUEEN OF AVALON (passive) Morgana

YOU MAY DEPLOY UNITS IN ANY REGION and MOVE THEM FROM UNEXPLORED REGIONS.

Lord Artifact Assignment

Excalibur and the Grail must be assigned to Morgana, increasing her Strength. They do NOT occupy an Artifact Slot; place them beside the board. Morgana has 3 Artifact Slots.

EASY-TO-FORGET RULES COMPENDIUM

Quick reference for the interactions that are easiest to forget and for situations that require a precise rules check.

Artifact Equipment

All Champions (1 slot) and Lords (2 slots) CAN equip Artifacts (exception: Fey Champions CANNOT equip Artifacts). Lord Artifacts (e.g. Excalibur, Grail) must be assigned to their respective Lord, increasing their Strength, but do NOT occupy an Artifact Slot (place them beside the board).

Failed Battle Plans

If the Combat Cards drawn exceed the Limit, the Battle Plan FAILS (you gain only half the Battle Plan's base Strength, rounded up). If you won the Battle, discard all cards; if you lost the Battle and FAILED the Battle Plan, return only the Bonus Cards to your hand; if you lost the Battle but DID NOT FAIL the Battle Plan, return all cards to your hand, both Battle Plan and Bonus Cards.

Abilities & Reaction Abilities

Reaction Abilities work identically to abilities BUT can also be activated outside your turn or the Preparation Phase. Both can be activated max 1 time per Season unless otherwise specified (e.g. “Once per Turn...”)

Artifact and Mythic Beast Abilities

Artifact abilities can be activated ONLY if Equipped (even if the Units carrying them are not deployed). Mythic Beast abilities can be activated EVEN if they are not deployed on Avalon.

Initiative and Exploration

Exploration is triggered ONLY during the Preparation Phase when a Lord moves or is deployed into an Unexplored Region (max 1 time per Turn). During the War Phase the Region remains unexplored.

Movement from an Unexplored Region

You cannot move Units from an Unexplored Region (exception: Morgana's “Fey Enchantment” ability).

Limit of 5 Units per Region

Limit of 5 Units per player per Region (checked at the end of the turn and during the War Phase). Settlements are NOT Units. The Ancient One (Side B) and the Green Knight COUNT as units on the board for the purpose of this limit.

Mythic Beast Limit

Limit of X Mythic Beasts, where X is the current Season. The Ancient One (Druids) and the Leviathan (Fomorians) do NOT COUNT toward the Mythic Beast Limit.

Movement Icon = Deployment Icon

You may treat ONE Movement Icon of your choice as the Lord's Deployment on the Map (if the card has multiple Movement Icons, only one of them becomes Lord Deployment).

Holy Grail

It may be used ONLY during the Preparation Phase (tie-break for Initiative or Bidding) and ONLY once per Season. During the Mist Phase, shuffle the Grail (CARD) back into the Mist Deck for Region setup BUT leave the Grail (OBJECT/TOKEN) with its owner, if claimed (it can therefore be used during that Season). It does NOT occupy an Artifact Slot.

Excalibur

It is obtained at the beginning of the War Phase by the Player furthest ahead on the Favor Track (in case of a tie, no one obtains it) or earlier if the last space of the Favor Track is reached. It does NOT occupy an Artifact Slot.

Green Knight

Obtained at the beginning of the War Phase (after Excalibur) by the Player furthest behind in Score if there are at least 2 Star Icons between them and the next Player with the lowest Score. It COUNTS toward the Maximum Unit Limit per Region (max 5).

Vernax & Questing Beast

They both COUNT AS CHAMPIONS. They therefore allow you to play Battle Plan Cards.

Prophecy Cards

When a Prophecy Card asks, for example, to “have 4 Strength in 3 Regions”, it ALWAYS means having AT LEAST that amount of Strength, not exactly that amount. Prophecy Cards can be completed ONLY during the Preparation Phase.

Tie Breaking

When a Tie occurs DURING THE PREPARATION PHASE, the tie-break order is: Bearer of the Grail (if tied) > Player With Lowest VPs > Faction With Lowest VPs On Board. (Note: The Bearer of The Grail chooses his Place in the Order of play). When a Tie occurs DURING THE WAR PHASE, the tie-break order is: Player With Lowest VPs > Faction With Lowest VPs On Board.

Exploration Timing

When you Explore a Region, resolve the effects in the following order: Move Into Region All Units (of that single Move/Deploy icon) > Reveal Tile and Mist Card > Gain Exploration Bonus > Bid for Mythical Beast (if present) > Resolve Remaining Icons on the Card.

Strength Tokens

When gaining a +1 Strength Token, put it in your Faction Board or Unit's card or the Settlement itself. They are considered PERMANENT and the resource UNLIMITED. They can be accumulated and used through Seasons. You don't have to spend them.

Cards Used as Payment

When committing Cards to Pay for another Card, you CANNOT USE THEM for other purposes (such as Bidding for Mythic Beasts). However, if you gain a discount before you resolve the card, you CAN RETURN ONE BACK into your hand.

Mistwalkers

Mistwalker Units may move from Unexplored Regions, including Regions with Deep Mist. Once per Turn, they may move for free from one Region: if they move INTO an Unexplored Region, DRAW A QUEST CARD and VIEW THE MIST CARD of that Region.

Quest Cards

You gain them ONLY WHEN your Mistwalker MOVES INTO an Unexplored Region (even with Deep Mist token). There are two types of Quest Cards: with an ABILITY (regular or reaction) or with an OBJECTIVE. Regular Ability can only be used once during Preparation Phase and then discarded; Reaction Ability triggers whenever you met the conditions and then discarded. The Objective type grants varied VPs as long as the REQUIREMENTS ARE MET and the Cards are IN YOUR HAND AT END OF SEASON, after which they are discarded. In alternative, ONLY IF you didn't use them for their Ability or their Objective, they GRANT 2/2/3 VPs at End of Season. Either way, they don't give the usual 1 VP at End of Season when discarding cards. (Exception: the Quest Cards "Veneration" and "Sanctify" grant VPs only for their Objective).

End of the English guide.
